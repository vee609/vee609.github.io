Privacy policy

Google data Mira accesses

With the operator's authorization, Mira can read and manage events on the operator's primary Google Calendar and access the operator's Google Tasks lists. Scheduling data can include titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, due dates, and completion status.

How Mira uses and shares data

Mira uses this data to answer scheduling questions, identify conflicts, and carry out changes the operator approves. Details needed to answer a request may be processed by the configured AI model provider and may appear in the operator's private Telegram chat. Approved changes are sent to Google Calendar or Google Tasks. Google scheduling data is used for the requested scheduling features and is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

OAuth credentials are stored on the private system that runs Mira. Scheduling details may be retained in conversation history and backups, and in messages sent to Telegram. The operator can remove records from the applicable service and remove local copies through the private system's administration tools. Deleting a record from one service does not remove copies from other services or existing backups.

The operator can revoke future access from their Google Account connections and remove the stored credential from the private system. For questions about data handling or deletion, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
